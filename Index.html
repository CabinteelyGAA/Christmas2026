From the Executive Committee

Christmas & New Year 2027 Message

A chairde,

As 2026 draws to a close, on behalf of the entire Cabinteely GAA & Camogie Executive Committee, we extend a heartfelt Nollaig Shona to all our players, volunteer coaches, parents, referees, sponsors, and supporters across Cabinteely, Killiney, Loughlinstown, and Foxrock.

From Saturday morning Nursery sessions at Kilbogget Park to thrilling championship battles in Gaelic football, hurling, and camogie, our club continues to thrive thanks to your generosity and community spirit.

Wishing every family a peaceful, joyful Christmas and a healthy, successful 2027. Cábán tSíle Abú!

Club Chairperson & Executive Cabinteely GAA & Camogie • Kilbogget Park
